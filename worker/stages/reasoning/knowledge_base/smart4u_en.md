## Websites

### WordPress websites
We build websites exclusively on WordPress, mainly using Elementor: one-page sites, business websites, multi-page sites, landing pages and larger websites. Additional modules such as forms, calendars or booking systems may be possible by individual arrangement. Our websites are adapted for mobile devices. Website development is priced individually once the scope, number of pages and required features are known, so we prepare an individual quote. Useful questions for the customer: is it a new website or a redesign, what industry, how many pages, what features, do they already have a domain, hosting, copy and photos, and what is the deadline.

### Hosting, domains and maintenance prices
Hosting costs PLN 250 net per year if the customer uses Smart4U hosting. A domain costs approximately PLN 100 net per year, depending on the domain. Optional website technical updates and backups cost PLN 49 net per month. Content changes and other commissioned work cost PLN 100 net per hour, and packages such as 2 or 4 hours may be available. Customers can edit content themselves in the WordPress CMS or ask Smart4U to do it. Example of all annual options combined: hosting 250 + domain 100 + legal package 399 + 12 x 49 maintenance = PLN 1,337 net per year. This is only a calculation of these components, not a mandatory package.

### Legal package
The legal package is a separate package costing PLN 399 net per year, including updates to legal documents. It covers GDPR, privacy policies, cookies, clauses and consent management mechanisms (CMP), as appropriate to the service scope. Buying the package does not automatically make the whole company fully legally compliant. For the most serious GDPR violations, regulations allow a maximum fine of up to EUR 20 million or 4% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher, but not every irregularity leads to such a fine.

## SCARD

### What SCARD is
SCARD is a digital business profile, not just a traditional business card. It presents services, contact details, photos and videos, social media, location, links and an option to save the contact. It can be shared by link, QR code and NFC, and no app installation is required. SCARD can complement an existing website; it does not replace it.

### SCARD price and what is included
SCARD costs PLN 399 net per year. The subscription includes creating the profile, maintenance and 24/7 availability, updates to details, offers, photos and videos by the Smart4U team, and an NFC sticker. Metal cards may cost extra and their price needs confirmation from the team. The customer does not need to design or configure anything: they provide basic company details, services and offer information, send photos if they have them, and if not, Smart4U can prepare the materials. The profile may be ready in as little as one day if the necessary information is received and the scope allows it. Updates are included in the subscription.
